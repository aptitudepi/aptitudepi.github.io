First Semester
Experience

My first semester at Texas A&M has been a mix of late-night traditions, intense labs, spontaneous adventures, and moments that felt like home away from home.

From Diwali lights to Midnight Yell, each memory here captures a different part of how college life has shaped my routine, friendships, and goals.

Moments That Mattered

A collection of experiences that defined my first semester

Chess Strategy

Getting a brilliant move on chess.com is one of those quiet but deeply satisfying victories, where careful planning finally pays off on the board.

Lab Activity

Engaging in a hands-on lab activity turned theory into something real, making concepts click in a way that lectures alone never could.

Microcontroller Programming

Working on microcontroller programming let hardware and code come together, showing how embedded systems can bring even small ideas to life.

Dorm Life Fun

Drawing riddles on a roommate's whiteboard outside their door added small moments of fun that made dorm life feel more like a community.

Unexpected Events

Having to evacuate because someone in Mosher Hall could not make popcorn in the microwave was chaotic, but it quickly turned into one of those stories everyone remembers and laughs about later.

Midnight Yell

Attending Midnight Yell for the first time showed how powerful Aggie traditions are, with the entire stadium echoing with energy and spirit.

Aggie Spirit

Another view from Midnight Yell captures the 12th Man in full force, reminding me what it feels like to be part of something bigger than myself.

Cybersecurity Conference

Going to a cybersecurity conference offered a preview of the field beyond the classroom, from talks by experts to conversations with professionals and peers.

Celebrating Diwali

Celebrating Diwali with friends brought a piece of home and culture onto campus, turning an ordinary evening into something bright, familiar, and meaningful.

Campus Views

Taking random pictures of a water tower might seem simple, but it reflects how everyday scenes on campus slowly become part of what feels like home.

Family Moments

Going to a wedding with my twin sister during the semester was a refreshing break, reconnecting with family and returning to campus with new energy.
